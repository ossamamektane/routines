Routines – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

Routines does not collect, send or sell any data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What is stored

Your tasks, check-offs, wake-up times and settings are saved only on your device, inside the app. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your phone. No push service or server is involved. You can turn them off in the app’s menu or in your phone’s Settings.

Backups

If you choose “Export backup”, the app creates a file and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes is up to you.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone.
